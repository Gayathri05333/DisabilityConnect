About DisabilityConnect

A simple mini project built to make everyday accessibility information easy to find.

DisabilityConnect is a web application designed to help people with disabilities easily find accessibility-related information and get emergency support when they need it most.

From locating wheelchair-accessible places and nearby hospitals to sharing your live location with emergency contacts in one tap, DisabilityConnect keeps essential support close at hand.
